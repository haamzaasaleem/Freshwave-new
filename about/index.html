About Fresh Wave

A Madera-based cleaning company for homes, businesses and short-term rentals, built on doing the job properly and doing it the same way every time.

Our story

Fresh Wave Cleaning Co. started with a simple goal: reliable, high-quality cleaning that makes a space feel fresh and welcoming. We saw a need for a dependable company that could handle homes, businesses and short-term rentals with the same care and consistency.

That commitment to helping clients keep clean, stress-free spaces has grown into a cleaning service focused on residential, commercial and Airbnb work. Whether it’s a family home, a professional workspace or a guest-ready rental, a clean environment makes a real difference to the people using it.

We take pride in thorough cleans, clear communication and personal service. Every space is treated with respect, and every client’s needs are handled professionally. As we grow, the goal hasn’t changed: to be the cleaning company our clients trust and feel comfortable recommending.

Mission & values

Our mission is to provide cleaning that goes beyond expectations. A clean space isn’t only about appearance. It’s about a place where people feel comfortable, healthy and at ease.

  • Integrity & honestyLasting client relationships are built on trust. We’re straightforward about what we’ll do and when.
  • ReliabilityConsistent service you can depend on, the standard on every visit, not only the first.
  • Attention to detailThe small things matter, so we make sure they get done.
  • Service around youEvery cleaning is tailored to the space and the people in it.
  • Eco-conscious where possibleWe use eco-friendly practices and products whenever we can, for cleaner and healthier spaces.
