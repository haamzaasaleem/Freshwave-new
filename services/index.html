Our services

Regular upkeep, deep cleans and short-term rental turnovers for homes, businesses and rentals around Madera and Fresno. Not sure what you need? Call and we’ll talk it through.

Residential cleaning

Houses, apartments, condos

Our residential cleaning keeps your home clean, comfortable and stress free. Every room gets attention: surfaces spotless, floors refreshed, kitchen and bathrooms properly sanitized.

We tailor the visit to your routine and preferences, whether that’s a regular weekly or bi-weekly clean or a single deep clean.

  • Routine maintenance or deep-clean options
  • Detailed kitchen & bathroom sanitizing
  • Dusting, vacuuming and floor care
  • Eco-friendly products available
  • Customized to your home’s needs

Commercial & office cleaning

Offices, lobbies, shared spaces

A clean workplace is healthier for your team and makes the right impression on visitors. We maintain lobbies, offices, common areas and restrooms with care and precision.

Small office or larger commercial space, we schedule around your hours so cleaning doesn’t get in the way of work.

  • Office, lobby & common-area upkeep
  • Restroom cleaning & sanitizing
  • Trash removal & surface disinfecting
  • Flexible scheduling for minimal disruption
  • A professional, welcoming workspace

Airbnb & short-term rental cleaning

Hosts & property managers

Reviews depend on the place being spotless when guests walk in. We handle quick turnovers between stays and deeper cleans when the property needs it.

Linens refreshed, surfaces sanitized, essentials restocked if you’d like, and everything reset to the layout your guests expect.

  • Fast, reliable turnover service
  • Fresh linens and bed resets
  • Restocking essentials on request
  • Full surface disinfecting & tidy-up
  • A guest-ready space every time

Move-in & move-out cleaning
